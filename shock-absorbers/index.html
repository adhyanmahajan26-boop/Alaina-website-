Shock absorbers for trucks and pickups

Alaina shock absorbers (shockers) cover front and rear axle applications for Mahindra Bolero, Bolero Pickup and Navi Star, plus truck and bus fitments for Tata and Ashok Leyland. Each unit is built to the OE reference length, stroke and valving of the part it replaces and is pressure-tested before dispatch.

When to replace a shocker

  • Oil leaking down the body.
  • The vehicle keeps bouncing after a speed breaker, or the tyres wear in patches.
  • Knocking from the axle, or a vehicle that sways and dives more than it used to.

How to order

  1. Find the part. Use the Alaina part number, the OE number on your old unit, or your vehicle model.
  2. Send it on WhatsApp to +91 79825 55636 with the quantity. A photo of the old unit helps.
  3. Get a quote and dispatch. We confirm price and stock, then dispatch across India through Mahajan Motors India, Kashmere Gate, Delhi.

Questions buyers ask

What does shocker mean?

In Indian trade, a shocker is a shock absorber. Alaina shockers include truck, pickup and car shock absorbers as well as cabin dampers.

Which Mahindra pickups do Alaina shockers fit?

Mahindra Bolero, Bolero Pickup and Navi Star, front and rear, plus the Bolero / Marshal steering damper.

How do I order Alaina shock absorbers?

WhatsApp the part number or your vehicle model and quantity to +91 79825 55636. We dispatch across India.
