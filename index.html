01 / 03 · Cabin dampers

Inside a cabin damper

  1. Under the cabin

    The cabin sits on dampers between it and the chassis. They take the jolts of the road before they reach the driver.

  2. 01

    Mounting eyes

    Bushed eyes at both ends carry the cabin's movement without knocking.

  3. 02

    Damper body

    Length and stroke are matched to the OE part number it replaces.

  4. 03

    Coil spring

    Holds the cabin at ride height, so the damper only has to control it.

  5. 04

    Piston rod and seal

    Oil, seals and bump rubbers are specified for overload and heat.

  6. 05

    Dust shield

    Keeps road grit off the rod and seal. The part number is stamped on every body.

  7. 06

    Tested as one unit

    Every unit is cycled on the rig and pressure-tested before dispatch.

02 / 03

Pickup and LCV shockers

Front and rear shockers and steering dampers for Mahindra pickups.

  1. 01

    Oil does the work

    A piston pushes oil through valves inside the tube, so a bump is absorbed instead of passed on to the load.

  2. 02

    Front, rear and steering

    Separate part numbers for the front and rear of Bolero, Bolero Pickup and Navi Star, and a steering damper for Bolero and Marshal.

  3. 03

    Fits like the original

    Length, stroke and mounts follow the part number it replaces.
