Manufacturing

How We Build It

Every Alaina unit goes through a ten-step controlled assembly sequence — verified at each stage before the next begins.

01 Strut Assembly
  1. Parts Ready

    Every precision component laid out — piston, seals, springs, mounts, and the damper body.

  2. Seal & Oil Seal

    High-performance oil seals pressed into the body, engineered for over a million cycles.

  3. Piston Rod Inserted

    Chrome-plated rod slid in with zero-tolerance alignment for consistent damping force.

  4. Coil Spring Fitted

    Precision-wound spring seated around the body, rated for the exact application load.

  5. Top Mount Fixed

    Top mount torqued to spec, completing the damper-to-chassis connection.

  6. Shocker Assembled

    Fully assembled unit ready for quality inspection before dispatch.

  7. Shocker Installed

    Unit mounted into the wheel arch, connecting knuckle, brake, and suspension geometry.

  8. Wheel Fitted

    Wheel refitted and torqued — shocker sits live in the suspension under full vehicle weight.

  9. Smooth Performance

    Damper absorbs every input — potholes, undulations, hard stops — invisibly and consistently.

  10. Driving Smoothly

    From raw components to confident driving. Kilometre after kilometre.

02 Cabin Damper Assembly
  1. Cylinder Prepared

    Outer tube precision-bored and cleaned — every micron matters for consistent damping over a million cycles.

  2. Inner Tube Inserted

    Twin-tube construction: inner cylinder pressed inside the outer, creating the primary working chamber.

  3. Oil Fill

    VG46 hydraulic oil filled to exact specification — volume and viscosity determine damping response.

  4. Piston Rod Inserted

    Hard-chrome rod — Ra ≤ 0.2μm — slid through the rod guide with zero-tolerance alignment.

  5. Seal Pack Pressed

    Dual-lip NBR seals torqued into position, rated for one million full-stroke cycles.

  6. Dust Boot Fitted

    TPR accordion boot snapped over the rod — blocking dust, water, and road debris from the seal face.

  7. Top Eye Mounted

    Forged mounting eye welded and pressure-tested at its rated cab-frame load capacity.

  8. Damper Complete

    Pressure-tested at 15 BAR. Zero leaks. Force curve verified. Ready for dispatch.

  9. Cab Mounted

    Unit bolted between cab floor bracket and chassis rail — isolating cab from engine vibration and road shock.

  10. Cabin Stable

    At speed, over potholes, under full payload — the Alaina cabin damper keeps the driver smooth. Every kilometre.
