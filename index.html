Forty-eight years of one obsession: the parts other people can't find, built properly.

Alaina began in 1978 supplying dampers to transporters who couldn't afford downtime. That discipline still runs the house — real stock, real part numbers, and a catalogue built from what Indian fleets actually run, not what's easy to make.

Today the range covers heavy-duty cabin dampers for every major truck platform, pickup and LCV shockers, and a rare-strut line that keeps discontinued imports on the road. Distributed nationally by Mahajan Motors India.
